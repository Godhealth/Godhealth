Privacy Policy · Version 1.0

Your privacy matters.

GodHealth uses your personal information carefully and only for the purposes described below.

What we collect

Depending on how you use GodHealth, we may collect your first name, email address, phone number, consent choices, Kingdom Vitality Scan answers and scores, report information, and details you submit when requesting a Strategy Call.

Why we collect it

  • To provide, generate and communicate your Kingdom Vitality Scan results and personal report.
  • To respond to your GodHealth Strategy Call request and contact you about that appointment.
  • To operate the services or resources you have requested.
  • If you actively opt in, to send GodHealth Insider updates, Biblical health insights, practical stewardship guidance and relevant offers.
  • To protect the security and reliability of our website and services.

Kingdom Vitality Scan content is educational and Biblical and science-informed. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Consent and communication

Scan communication, Strategy Call contact and optional marketing are recorded as separate consent choices. Agreeing to receive a requested scan or call response does not automatically subscribe you to general marketing. Marketing is optional and is not pre-selected.

Storage and service providers

Information is stored in Supabase and may be processed by trusted service providers that help us deliver reports, email communication and scheduling. Access is limited to what is needed to provide these services. Private report files are not placed in a public storage bucket.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in an email. You may also ask us to update your communication preferences, access or correct your information, withdraw marketing consent, or request deletion where applicable.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, legal obligations and legitimate business records. We use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as GodHealth develops. The current version will remain available on this page.

Contact GodHealth

For privacy questions, consent changes or deletion requests, email info@godhealth.org.
