A SIMPLE WAY TO BEGIN

Start a conversation.
Make one action easier.

A busy church calendar can leave food, movement and rest behind. These resources give your group a practical starting point without needing to create a health program.

Share the free Blueprint.

Invite people to request their own workbook and bonus scan. Each person chooses whether to share their results.

Choose an ordinary-week action.

Use a guide to discuss a manageable walk, a planned meal or a realistic evening routine. Adapt it to ability and circumstances.

Encourage a return after setbacks.

Agree on a supportive check-in. Ask what helped and what needs adjusting, without comparing bodies or judging faith.

THE IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

Are the free resources available to our church?

Yes. Share the GodHealth resource and signup links so individuals can request their own materials. Contact us about other uses or specific needs before adapting or republishing the content.

Do you offer a separate group coaching program here?

This page provides a starting point for using free resources. The paid coaching described on this website is personal, one-to-one online coaching. Contact GodHealth to discuss a specific church enquiry.

Should people share their scan results with a leader?

Only if they choose to. Scan results are personal. Avoid collecting sensitive health information as part of an informal group activity.
