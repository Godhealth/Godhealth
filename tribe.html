The Weekly Rhythm

Every week has a ritual.
Every ritual is rooted in Scripture.

Monday
Monday Mission
Robin posts one question: "Who are you bringing into the Tribe this week?" Tribe members respond. Community grows.
Wednesday
The Mid-Week Word
One verse. One question from Robin. Members reflect and respond to each other.
Friday
Win of the Week
The community nominates each other. Robin chooses the winner and honours them publicly in the Hall of Faithfulness.
Saturday
The Sabbath + 24-Hour Fast
Rest. Fast. No phone. No food from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset. The community is silent. The day belongs to God alone.
Sunday
The Sunday Intention
One verse. One weekvision. One intention question. One concrete action. Members fill in the Weekly Vision Template and share with their Covenant Partner.
The Core Culture

We do not walk alone.

The Tribe is not just a community where you happen to be with other believers. It is a community where people bring each other. Where you are responsible for one person beside you.

"Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour. For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up."

Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 — King James Version
01
Your First Role
The One Who Was Brought

Someone brought you here. God placed GodHealth on your path. You are the one who was brought. Welcome. You are exactly where you are meant to be.

02
Your Second Role
The One Who Brings

You are responsible for one person. A friend. A family member. Someone whose Temple, Soul, and Spirit you want to see transformed. You bring them. You say: come with me.

03
Your Third Role
The One Who Holds

You are the accountability partner of the person you brought. On the days they want to quit — you hold them. On the days you want to quit — they hold you. That is covenant.
