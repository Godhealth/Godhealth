Cohort Model

Built For Structure, Support And Lasting Change.

The GodHealth Transformation Journey is delivered through structured 12-week cohorts. A new cohort begins every six weeks, while a maximum of two active cohorts are supported at one time.

This protects the quality of the journey and helps every participant receive clear structure, accountability and personal support.
