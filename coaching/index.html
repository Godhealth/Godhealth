Questions

Coaching FAQ

Is this only for Christians who want to lose weight?+
Weight loss is a common starting point, but the deeper goal is Body, Soul and Spirit stewardship through Christ-centered lifestyle change.
Is this a diet?+
No. It is a coaching journey built around nutrition, movement, recovery, habits, faith and accountability.
What happens during the Pre-Season?+
You prepare your environment, complete your intake, set your baseline and begin simple starter routines before Day One.
How much live support is included?+
Each cohort is designed to include live workouts, masterclasses, Q&A, scorecards and accountability. Exact details may vary by cohort.
What happens if I fall behind?+
The journey includes reset tools and accountability so you can return to your next right action without shame.
Is the program suitable for women and men?+
Yes. The coaching principles are practical and adaptable. For pregnancy, eating disorders, medication, chronic conditions or complex health concerns, speak with a qualified healthcare professional first.
What happens after the 12 weeks?+
The final phase helps you build a continuation plan and a sustainable minimum lifestyle you can carry forward.
