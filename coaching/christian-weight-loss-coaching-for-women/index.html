THE IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

Do I need to be fit before I join?

No. Robin adapts your starting plan to your current ability, schedule and available equipment. You do not need to lose weight first or follow an athlete’s training routine.

Can this work around caring responsibilities?

Yes. We look at work, family commitments and the time you can realistically use. Your plan includes a shorter busy-day option. We confirm suitable virtual appointment times before you join.

What about pregnancy, postpartum recovery or menopause?

Tell us before joining. GodHealth provides lifestyle coaching, not obstetric, hormonal or specialist medical care. Relevant healthcare clearance or specialist support may be needed so your food and exercise plan suits your circumstances.

Who will coach me?

Robin Kramps provides your weekly lifestyle coaching and private online PT. Patricia provides monthly private Christian mindset coaching and visualization. All sessions are virtual.
