GodHealth uses your personal information carefully and only for the purposes described below.

What we collect

Depending on how you use GodHealth, we may collect your first name, email address, phone number, consent choices, Kingdom Vitality Scan answers and scores, report information, and details you submit when requesting a Strategy Call. If you allow optional cookies, we also collect online identifiers and usage data: the pages you visit, the buttons you use, the campaign that brought you here, your device and browser type, your IP address, your approximate location and cookie identifiers. When you request the free Blueprint, we store with your request the page you signed up on and the campaign link that brought you there (campaign parameters and, if present, the ad click identifier), whatever your cookie choice.

Why we collect it

  • To provide, generate and communicate your Kingdom Vitality Scan results and personal report.
  • To respond to your GodHealth Strategy Call request and contact you about that appointment.
  • To operate the services or resources you have requested.
  • If you actively opt in, to send GodHealth Insider updates, Biblical health insights, practical stewardship guidance and relevant offers.
  • If you allow statistics cookies, to understand how our websites are used and to improve them.
  • If you allow advertising cookies, to measure and improve our advertising on Facebook and Instagram.
  • To protect the security and reliability of our website and services.

Kingdom Vitality Scan content is educational and Biblical and science-informed. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Consent and communication

Scan communication, Strategy Call contact and optional marketing are recorded as separate consent choices. Agreeing to receive a requested scan or call response does not automatically subscribe you to general marketing. Marketing is optional and is not pre-selected.

Cookies and browser storage

Optional statistics and advertising cookies are off until you opt in. You can accept or reject them, choose them separately, or reopen Cookie preferences at the bottom of the page at any time. Your choice applies to all GodHealth websites (www.godhealth.org, blueprint.godhealth.org and sales.godhealth.org). Rejecting optional cookies does not block the website, video or contact options.

  • Essential preference cookie: gh_cookie_consent remembers your choices for up to six months. It records the selected categories and when you chose them, not your name, email or health information.
  • Statistics (Google Analytics 4): if you opt in, Google Analytics, a service of Google Ireland Limited, sets the cookies _ga and _ga_6SWE12FJQ7 (up to two years) and records which pages you visit, which buttons you use, sign-ups and purchases (with the order amount), your device and browser type, your approximate location and how you reached us. We do not send your name, email address, phone number, scan answers or scores to Google Analytics. We keep this data for 14 months.
  • Advertising (Meta): if you opt in, the Meta Pixel, a service of Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, sets the cookies _fbp and _fbc (up to 90 days; _fbc holds the click id of the ad you followed) and tells Meta which GodHealth pages you view and when you start a contact, sign up, start a checkout or buy, so we can measure and improve our ads on Facebook and Instagram. Sign-ups, contact clicks, started checkouts and purchases are also sent to Meta from our server (Meta Conversions API), together with your IP address, browser type and these cookie identifiers. When you request the free Blueprint or complete a purchase, a scrambled (hashed) version of your email address is included so Meta can match that action to an ad. Meta can link this information to your Facebook or Instagram account. Because GodHealth is a Christian health service, it can show an interest in faith and health. We never send scan answers, scores or anything you write to Meta. For this processing GodHealth and Meta are joint controllers; see Meta’s Controller Addendum and Meta’s Privacy Policy.
  • Campaign details: if you opt in to advertising, campaign identifiers from the link you followed are kept in session storage (godhealth_public_attribution_v1 on www.godhealth.org, godhealth_blueprint_campaign on blueprint.godhealth.org) during the current browser tab session and carried to GodHealth booking and resource links. They are cleared when you withdraw this permission. When you request the free Blueprint after opting in, a short note (gh_track_pending: a random event number and, for advertising, the hashed email address) is kept in session storage until the confirmation page opens (it is ignored after 30 minutes and gone when you close the tab), so that your request is counted once on the confirmation page.

Google and Meta may process this information in the United States. When you withdraw a permission, we stop sending data and remove the related cookies from your browser. Your consent to cookies is separate from consent to email marketing, a coaching enquiry or a scan. Our introduction video is hosted with our website. The WhatsApp invitation does not load a chat SDK: WhatsApp opens only when you choose its contact link.

Storage and service providers

Information is stored in Supabase and may be processed by trusted service providers that help us deliver reports, email communication and scheduling. Access is limited to what is needed to provide these services. Private report files are not placed in a public storage bucket. If you allow optional cookies, Google (statistics) and Meta (ad measurement) receive the information described under Cookies and browser storage.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in an email. You may also ask us to update your communication preferences, access or correct your information, withdraw marketing consent, or request deletion where applicable. You can change or withdraw your cookie choices at any time through Cookie preferences at the bottom of the page. If you believe we handle your information incorrectly, you may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, legal obligations and legitimate business records. We use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as GodHealth develops. The current version will remain available on this page.
