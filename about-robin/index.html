THE PERSON BEHIND THE PLAN

Elite-sport experience.
Applied to everyday life.

Competitive hurdles taught me the value of preparation, progression and consistent practice. Placing second at the Dutch National Hurdles Championships is part of my story. It is not the standard I expect a busy parent to train toward.

My work as a Certified Lifestyle Coach Level 4, following my CIOS education in Sport, Movement and Health, is about translating good principles into something a real person can follow.

I have trained, coached or supported more than 200 people. At GodHealth, personal attention matters: your schedule, your starting ability and the problems you run into between sessions.
