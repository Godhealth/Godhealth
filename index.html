Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about GodHealth.

Do I need to be very religious to join GodHealth?+
GodHealth is built for Christians who believe in Jesus Christ and want to honour God with their health. You do not need to be perfect. You need a heart that is open and a desire to grow. Come as you are.
Is the Tribe really free? What is the catch?+
The GodHealth Tribe is completely free — no credit card, no trial, no hidden cost. It will stay free. The Inner Covenant is the paid program. The Tribe exists because Robin believes no Christian should walk this road alone regardless of financial situation.
How much does the Inner Covenant cost?+
The investment for the Inner Covenant is never shown publicly. It is discussed personally in a call with our team — because you are not a transaction, you are a person. Apply and our team will contact you within 24 hours.
What is the difference between the three offers?+
The Arise Challenge is your first 7 days — free, automated, Biblical, powerful. The Tribe is the free permanent community with daily encouragement, weekly teachings, and Biblical rituals. The Inner Covenant is the 90-day personal coaching program with Robin — your own plan, direct access, live sessions twice a week. All three are connected.
Can I ask Robin questions directly in the Tribe?+
No. In the Tribe, Robin posts and members respond and talk to each other. Direct questions to Robin are exclusively for Inner Covenant members. This is the boundary that makes the Covenant genuinely valuable.
Is the Sabbath fast mandatory?+
The Saturday Sabbath and 24-Hour Fast is a communal invitation — not a law. People with medical conditions fast 12 hours instead of 24, or choose a different form of Sabbath rest. It is between you and God.
I am not in the Netherlands. Can I still join?+
Absolutely. GodHealth is built for English-speaking believers worldwide. The Inner Covenant sessions are online — Robin coaches you live via video regardless of where you are in the world.
What happens after the Inner Covenant ends?+
After 90 days you receive a personal review with Robin, a Transformation Certificate, and permanent Founding Member status in the Tribe. Three months later there is a live Alumni Reunion. You become a mentor for new Tribe members and automatically reach Level V — The Pillar.
