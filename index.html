Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Kingdom Vitality Scan really free?+
Yes — completely free. It takes about 10 minutes, needs no payment details, and you receive your Body, Soul & Spirit scores and personalized next steps right away.
What is the Alignment Gap™?+
The distance between how your body, soul and spirit are functioning today and how they were designed to work together. The scan helps you identify possible areas for practical stewardship.
Is this medical advice?+
No. GodHealth provides education, accountability and lifestyle coaching — not medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency care. For any medical concern, please speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
Do I need to be a "strong" Christian to take part?+
Not at all. GodHealth is built for believers at every stage of the walk. Come as you are — the scan meets you where you are and points you back toward God's design for your body, soul and spirit.
What exactly do I receive after the scan?+
Your Body, Soul and Spirit scores, an Overall Alignment Score, your Primary Alignment Gap, personalized next steps and practical stewardship recommendations.
What is the GodHealth Strategy Call?+
It is a complimentary, personal conversation to get to know each other, discuss your challenges and goals, explain the 12-week GodHealth Coaching Journey, and explore whether the next cohort may be the right fit. You may book the call directly without completing the scan first.
Will my information stay private?+
Yes. Your details are used only to deliver your results and, if you choose to opt in, to share relevant coaching options. Privacy protected, no spam, and you can withdraw consent at any time.
Can I take part from outside the Netherlands?+
Yes. GodHealth serves English-speaking believers around the world, and the coaching is delivered online — so you can begin wherever you are.
