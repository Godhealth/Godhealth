✦ Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about
GodHealth.

Do I need to be very religious to join GodHealth?+
GodHealth is built for Christians — people who believe in Jesus Christ and want to honour God with their health. You do not need to be perfect or have all the answers. You need a heart that is open and a desire to grow. Come as you are.
Is the Tribe really free? What is the catch?+
The GodHealth Tribe is completely free — no credit card, no trial period, no hidden cost. It will stay free. The Inner Covenant is the paid program. The Tribe exists because Robin believes no Christian should walk this road alone — regardless of financial situation.
How much does the Inner Covenant cost?+
The investment for the Inner Covenant is never shown publicly. It is discussed personally in a call with our team — because you are not a transaction. You are a person with a specific situation. Apply below and our team will contact you within 24 hours to have that conversation.
What is the difference between the Arise Challenge, the Tribe, and the Inner Covenant?+
The Arise Challenge is your first 7 days — free, automated, Biblical, powerful. The Tribe is the free permanent community where you continue growing with daily encouragement, weekly teachings, and Biblical rituals. The Inner Covenant is the 90-day personal coaching program with Robin — your own plan, direct access, live sessions twice a week. All three are connected. Many members start with the Challenge, stay in the Tribe, and apply for the Covenant when they are ready.
Can I ask Robin questions directly in the Tribe?+
No. In the Tribe, Robin posts and members respond and talk to each other. Direct questions to Robin are exclusively for Inner Covenant members. This is not a restriction — it is a boundary that protects the quality of Robin's attention and makes the Inner Covenant genuinely valuable. The Tribe is built around community — believers supporting each other.
What does "Holy Stretching and Breathwork" mean? Is it yoga?+
It is not yoga. Never yoga. GodHealth's Holy Stretching and Breathwork is fully Biblical — rooted in Scripture, built on the science of physical recovery and nervous system regulation. The movements honour the body as God's temple. There are no Eastern spiritual elements. None.
Is the Sabbath fast mandatory?+
The Saturday Sabbath and 24-Hour Fast is a communal invitation — not a law. Nobody is forced. People with medical conditions or health concerns fast 12 hours instead of 24, or choose a different form of Sabbath rest. The community participates together, and Robin does not police participation. It is between you and God.
I am not in the Netherlands. Can I still join?+
Absolutely. GodHealth is built for English-speaking believers worldwide. The Tribe has members from multiple countries. The Inner Covenant sessions are online — Robin coaches you live via video regardless of where you are in the world.
Can I join the Inner Covenant as a duo?+
Yes. The Inner Covenant is available for individuals and for duos — couples, close friends, or siblings who want to walk the 90 days together. The investment is higher for a duo and is discussed in the personal call. Both members receive everything the Inner Covenant offers, and they become each other's primary Covenant Pair.
What happens after the Inner Covenant ends?+
After 90 days you receive a personal review with Robin, a Transformation Certificate, and permanent Founding Member status in the Tribe. Three months later there is a live Alumni Reunion. You also become a mentor for new Tribe members and automatically reach Level V — The Pillar. The Inner Covenant does not end at Day 90. It becomes the beginning of the next chapter.
