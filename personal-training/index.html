COMMON QUESTIONS

Your questions, answered.

Where do you train?

I come directly to your home in Amsterdam. Together we agree on training times that fit your schedule and my availability.

Can I receive coaching in Dutch or English?

Absolutely. I offer personal training and coaching in both Dutch and English. Choose whichever language feels most comfortable for you.

Do I need a home gym?

No. We discuss your available space and equipment and adapt your training accordingly.

Can I start as a beginner?

Yes. Training is adapted to your experience, ability and goals.

What does the package include?

Four private sessions per month at your home, personalized training and nutrition plans, lifestyle guidance, accountability and access to the GodHealth coaching app.

What is the investment?

This is a premium private coaching service. We discuss the package, total price including applicable VAT and terms during your complimentary consultation.

What happens during the consultation?

We discuss your goals, schedule and training location in a free 30-minute conversation, in Dutch or English. There is no obligation to join.
