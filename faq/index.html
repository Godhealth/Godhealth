BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Your questions.
Clear answers.

Understand your complete online lifestyle support, the instant 12-week starting plan, the $250 first month and optional next 60 days and how we help you stay consistent.

Who is GodHealth coaching for?

Busy Christian men and women who want to lose excess fat, regain energy, stay strong as they age and finally stay consistent. We help you honor God with your body through simple routines that fit work, family and faith. Beginners are welcome.

What makes this different from a workout app?

The Kingdom Capacity Method combines ancient Biblical principles, modern health science and elite athlete expertise. Robin reviews your actual schedule and options, then refines your personalized lifestyle, training and nutrition plans—including sleep, daily rhythm, movement, mindset and discipline. Weekly virtual coaching and PT, monthly mindset sessions and daily accountability help you follow through.

What personalized plans do I receive?

Your personalized 12-week transformation lifestyle plan, a personalized Biblical and science-based nutrition plan, and a personalized fat loss and muscle-building training plan for your busy schedule. Robin reviews your actual work and family schedule, available time, equipment, preferences, budget and starting ability, then refines a plan you can keep following.

Is the full GodHealth Nutrition Bundle included?

Yes. Coaching includes the full Recipe Vault with 305 recipes across 7 recipe packs, plus the Biblical Nutrition Guide, 7-Day Transformation Blueprint, 30-Day Fasting & Done-for-You Meal Plan, 30-Day Kingdom Health Tracker, Food Swap Guide, Emergency Eating Guide and Quick-Start Assessment.

How long are the workouts?

Standard workouts and your weekly online personal-training session are designed for 30 minutes or less. No equipment is required. We can adapt training for a gym or adjust the volume when that suits your goals, ability and recovery.

What is the starting investment?

Normally $350 USD, now $250 USD for your first 30 days. Offer valid through December 31, 2026. Limited to 10 clients per GodHealth coaching journey. One-to-one coaching, with maximum personal attention for each client. After 30 days, you choose whether to continue with us for the remaining 60 days if the support feels right for you. We explain continuation pricing and terms before you choose. The discovery call is free.

How do I get started?

Book a free virtual discovery call. We discuss your goals, routine and obstacles, explain the starting arrangement and agree on next steps. Complete your Kingdom Capacity Assessment, then immediately generate and download your personalized 12-week transformation lifestyle plan PDF. It brings Biblical principles and scientifically supported health practices into a starting plan shaped by your answers and schedule. Robin reviews and refines your plans with you.

Do I have to continue after the first 30 days?

You choose. Normally $350 USD, now $250 USD for your first 30 days. Offer valid through December 31, 2026. Limited to 10 clients per GodHealth coaching journey. One-to-one coaching, with maximum personal attention for each client. If you like the support, decide whether to continue for the remaining 60 days. We explain the next-stage pricing and terms before you make that decision.

What happens on the free discovery call?

We talk about your goals, your schedule and what has made progress difficult. You can ask questions about support, time and pricing. If coaching is a suitable fit, we explain how to get started. There is no obligation to join.

Will I have to follow an extreme diet or fast?

Fasting and intermittent fasting can be included when suitable for your health and circumstances. They are personalized options, not requirements. No extreme diet is required. We create a personal eating plan around your goals, schedule and needs. GodHealth’s faith-based food principle is God-created food, not man-created food. We distinguish that conviction from scientific claims and consider medical needs and preferences.

What does Body, Soul and Spirit mean here?

Body covers food, movement, sleep and recovery. Soul covers habits, stress and the patterns that make consistency harder. Spirit connects your health choices with your Christian faith, purpose and rhythms. Your body size and health do not measure your faith or your worth.

Is GodHealth a medical service?

No. GodHealth offers lifestyle coaching and educational resources, not medical diagnosis, treatment, psychotherapy or emergency care. The assessments are lifestyle tools. Persistent fatigue, pain, symptoms or questions about medication should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

Can I start without paying for coaching?

Yes. The free 7-Step Fat Loss Blueprint is a self-guided starting point. Its bonus Kingdom Vitality Scan helps you reflect on your lifestyle. You can also read the practical guides in our resources library. No purchase is required to use those free resources.

Can I join from the United States?

Coaching is delivered online in English. Tell us your location and time zone on the discovery call so we can confirm workable session times before you join. GodHealth is registered in the Netherlands.

Do you guarantee a weight-loss result?

No. Your results depend on your starting point, health, circumstances and the actions you can sustain. We focus on an appropriate personal plan, support, feedback and measurable progress—not a guaranteed number on the scale.

Is everything virtual?

Yes. Your weekly coaching calls, weekly private personal training and monthly mindset sessions all take place online. Your personal training plan can be followed at home or in the gym. We confirm a schedule for your time zone before you join.

Who provides the coaching?

Robin Kramps is your weekly lifestyle coach and online personal trainer: CIOS Level 4 Certified Lifestyle Coach and Dutch Hurdles Silver Medalist. Your monthly private mindset coaching and visualization are with Patricia, a Christian success mindset trainer and public speaker who has spoken before 15,000+ people at Ahoy Rotterdam.

What happens immediately after the assessment?

You can generate and download your personalized 12-week transformation lifestyle plan PDF straight away. It connects your lifestyle patterns and obstacles with a practical plan for food, training, sleep and daily routines, based on your goals and busy week. Biblical principles provide the foundation; health science informs the actions. Robin then reviews and refines the starting plan with you.

How do you help me stay consistent?

We turn your goals into a few clear daily actions, a normal-day plan and a shorter busy-day backup. Daily accountability, reminders and access to Robin support you between sessions. Weekly coaching helps you review obstacles and adjust your plan instead of starting over.

What do you mean by longevity?

We mean caring for your long-term health: building strength, fitness, mobility, recovery and habits you can keep. It is about staying capable for your family, work and calling as you age. It is not a promise of a longer lifespan or a medical treatment.
